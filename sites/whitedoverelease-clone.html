White Dove Release

Offering the most affordable dove releases to LA, Orange and surrounding counties since 1989.

Few things stop a crowd the way a flock of white doves does, climbing into the sky all at once at a wedding or a memorial. It is the kind of moment people compare to a dolphin leaping at Sea World, or a bald eagle circling an NFL stadium before kickoff — and just like those moments, guests always ask the same question afterward: how do the birds find their way back home?

Few animals carry deeper symbolism than a white dove. At a wedding, they represent new beginnings and lasting love. At a memorial, they represent peace and a gentle farewell.

Serving Los Angeles & Orange County

White Dove Release serves Los Angeles and Orange Counties, along with parts of Riverside and San Bernardino Counties. Release packages are available for weddings, funerals, and other special occasions, along with smaller doves available in display cages.

Our History

Since 1989, Pastor Luthor Nelson and his wife, Susie, have released white homing doves at congregation events. (The birds are actually a breed of pigeon that looks like a dove.) Over the years, White Dove Release has appeared at a Dodger Stadium opening day, on Good Morning America, My Fair Wedding, Battle of the Wedding Designers, Semi-Homemade Wedding, and in a number of commercials and music videos.
